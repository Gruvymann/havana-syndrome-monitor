# Havana Syndrome Monitor dataset

Updated 2026-11-14T11:33:36.991103+00:00. 3754 items. https://gruvymann.github.io/havana-syndrome-monitor/

No license has been granted for reuse yet. Headlines and the linked articles remain the property of their publishers; the dataset holds titles, links, dates and automatically generated labels only (no article text).

Labels (framing, topics, claims, places, people, reliability, story and copy groups) are produced by keyword matching and text similarity, and machine translations are automatic: verify against the originals before relying on them. See the About page for methods and limits.

Fields: id, date, added, type, source, country, language, title, title_en, url, archive_url, story_id, wire_agency, copy_group, state_media, outlet_reliability, framing, topics, places, people_orgs, claims, video, link_status, correction_note
